# Salt levels: standard amounts

Generated by `node scripts/build_salt_levels.mjs --write`. Low salt = the recipe as written (blood pressure, heart or kidney disease). Standard = healthy adults, WHO < 5 g salt a day: main meals ~650–700 mg sodium a serving (cap 750), savoury breakfasts and bread ≤ 400 mg, sides ~200 mg, snacks ~150 mg. A recipe already near or above its target still gets a small step (a main +80 mg or more, never over 900 mg; the others in proportion). Only plain salt (or the recipe's own herb or black salt) is raised; canned beans, broth, soy sauce, miso and bouillon stay as written. Health labels are judged on the low-salt version.
