# Fredheim finite plant-based FODMAP feasibility audit

Date: 8 November 2026 (Europe/Madrid). Research and original drafts only; no repository changes.

## Decision

Eight original exact-input recipe drafts created: two breakfasts and six mains. ZERO are clinically reviewed or approved low-FODMAP. This is genuine ingredient-level audit progress, not a therapeutic menu ready to ship. A finite curated route remains feasible; current evidence gaps are itemized below.

## Evidence actually inspected

Article: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s40795-020-00374-3

Complete supplement downloaded/read: https://static-content.springer.com/esm/art%3A10.1186%2Fs40795-020-00374-3/MediaObjects/40795_2020_374_MOESM1_ESM.pdf

Article explicitly permits reuse under CC BY 4.0 and applies CC0 to data unless otherwise credited. Supplement has source references but no separate restrictive reuse credit line found. Retain author/DOI attribution and modification note anyway. This does not grant reuse of the unpublished extended database or proprietary upstream collections.

The paper compiled 1,060 foods, 75% values directly measured; the full database is request-only. Public supplement contains selected top-food lists, not a complete classification table. Combined polyols cannot support independent sorbitol/mannitol assignment. Methods include substitutions and calculated recipes. No serving safety thresholds were established here.

### Findings important to avoid false reassurance

- Potato and cucumber have full-looking rows, but their zero cells are compiled assignments: do NOT treat as independently measured zeros for every subtype, every form or every portion.

- Orange row includes 0.03 g fructan/100 g; this is composition, not a low-FODMAP threshold.

- Boiled green lentils appear with GOS 0.46 g/100 g only. No conclusion about other subtypes or the proposed 40 g portion follows.

- Table 5 assigns almond, sunflower and linseed GOS from an LSA mixture; this is not direct single-seed evidence. We did not transfer this value to pumpkin seeds.

- Soy bean or soy flour data cannot be transferred to firm tofu or fermented tempeh. Rice/oats/quinoa/polenta absent from these selected tables are UNKNOWN here, not FODMAP-free.

- References in a reusable article establish provenance, not automatic rights to copy the cited source in bulk.

## Nutrition and practical menu gaps

No energy, protein or micronutrient calculation performed; all nutrient fields null. Protein sources drafted: firm calcium-set tofu, plain soy tempeh, small boiled lentil portion, peanut butter and pumpkin seeds. These are distinct food forms, not assurance of adequate protein. Several depend on soy. A soy exclusion could remove most main-meal options; do not substitute automatically or silently offer a deficient week.

Two breakfasts and six mains are an audit set, not adequate weekly variety. Complete-day review must include energy/protein, iron, calcium, B12, iodine, zinc, omega-3, fibre/fluids and suitable supplementation/fortification as clinically needed. Product labels and valid nutrient data required. Fortified foods must also receive FODMAP ingredient review.

## What a qualified human reviewer must do

1. Dietitian/clinician with IBS/FODMAP expertise selects suitable adults and reviews red flags, coeliac assessment before gluten restriction, eating-disorder risk, pregnancy/children and nutritional vulnerabilities.

2. Resolve each ingredient state/brand to applicable lawful analytical data or suitably authorized professional assessment; establish serving assessment criteria, uncertainty and full-meal exposure. No AI inference substituted for review.

3. Assess complete fixed-serving recipes and actual day/week outputs. Sign and date review with source/version, allowed substitutions, evidence status and limitations. No Monash endorsement/certification implied.

4. Kitchen-test original drafts: record cooked weights/yield and realistic preparation. Portion scaling for appetite is not unrestricted: approve variants separately.

5. Ensure existing exclusions can still produce sufficient nutrition and variety. If not, block generation with specific shortfall rather than relax exclusions.

## Reintroduction requirements, not dose tables

Public clinical context: https://www.monashfodmap.com/blog/reintroduction-using-diary-function/ and https://www.monashfodmap.com/blog/practical-tips-fodmap-reintroduction/

Principles paraphrased only: one food/subgroup first, controlled baseline, progressive portions around three days, stop for unpleasant symptoms, recovery of 2–3 days or until settled. Clinician specifies exact food/form/doses; none generated in candidates.json. Record last observed tolerated and symptom-provoking amounts separately; do not infer a permanent maximum or diagnosis. Never home-challenge suspected allergy.

NHS gentle protocol exists: https://www.uhs.nhs.uk/Media/UHS-website-2019/Patientinformation/Digestionandurinaryhealth/Gentle-low-FODMAP-diet-3924-PIL.pdf — public access and suggested schedules do not establish redistribution rights or isolated-subgroup validity. No table copied.
